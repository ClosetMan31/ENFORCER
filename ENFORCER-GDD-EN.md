## 4. Documentation

### 4.1 Concept
ENFORCER is a 2D pixel-art run-and-gun game. It is built on dynamic gameplay, in which the player takes on the role of a police officer in the dangerous environment of Tanzer City. The concept combines simple rules with an increasingly complex world.

### 4.2 Plot
The plot provides the backdrop for the player’s actions. The story takes place in North America in the year 2060. Due to widespread corruption in the Free City of Tanzer, law enforcement agencies and corporations turn a blind eye to criminal activity occurring throughout the territory. The player takes on the role of Randall Bennett, a police officer working in an independent special unit of the Tanzer City Police Department. The general outline involves crisis situations in which the player acts as a law enforcement officer tasked with eliminating threats.

### 4.3 Game Structure
The game’s structure is based on a level-based system. Each section of gameplay introduces a different scenario, different enemies, or different mechanics. The game allows for a smooth transition between stages and a gradual increase in difficulty.

### 4.4 Player
The player takes on the role of a character with a fixed set of skills and weapons that can be upgraded throughout the game. The character has specific statistics and the ability to interact with the world.

### 4.5 Gameplay
Game mechanics include movement, interacting with objects, combat, and completing tasks. The gameplay system should be intuitive while leaving room for the development of more advanced features in the future.

### 4.6 Objectives
The game’s objectives include completing missions, eliminating threats, or achieving specific results. Above all, completing the story. The player should have a clear understanding of their current task and the long-term goals of the game.

## 5. Frontend

### 5.1 Introduction
In addition to the standard “Made in Unity” screen, the game will also feature a title screen, after which the player will be taken to the main menu.

### 5.2 Menu
The main menu contains basic options: start game, settings, exit, and, optionally, access to the player’s profile. The settings submenu mainly includes audio and controls, as well as two language options: Polish and English.

---

## 6. Gameplay

### 6.1 World
The game world is divided into areas of varying character. Each area has unique visual elements and a set of interactions. The world should be coherent and logical in its design.

### 6.2 View
The game uses a 2D side-view perspective. The camera should ensure good visibility of the surroundings and interactive objects. The background will vary depending on the current level the player is on.

### 6.3 Surfaces
Surfaces include various types of terrain, obstacles, and environmental elements. They can affect the movement of the player or enemies. The design of surfaces should support the clarity and dynamics of gameplay.

### 6.4 Object Types
Objects are divided into interactive, decorative, functional, and enemies. Each type has specific properties and behaviors. The object system should be easy to extend.

### 6.5 Enemies
Enemies pose the main threat to the player. Each enemy type has unique behaviors, statistics, and attack patterns. Their design should support a variety of challenges


### 6.6 Controls
- [A/D] - Move
- [S] - Crouch
- [Space] - Jump
- [F] - Shoot
- [R] - Switch weapon
